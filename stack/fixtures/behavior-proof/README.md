# Behavior-Proof Fixtures

These fixtures exercise the V2 route from established diagnosis to proportionate
behavior proof, local refactoring, conditional full refinement, and safe
delegation.

They validate portable outcomes rather than agent-specific commands:

- an already-requested fix continues after root-cause diagnosis;
- the proof surface is the smallest stable observable boundary;
- non-TDD proof records why automated TDD is unsuitable;
- refactor-after-green stays local to TDD while full refinement is conditional;
- delegation starts only from a concrete board with independent write ownership
  and settled shared interfaces.
